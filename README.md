# Hi there, I'm Sphesihle Mkhungo 👋

## Agricultural Biostatistician | Data Analyst | Aspiring Data Scientist

Welcome to my GitHub portfolio!

I am an Agricultural Biostatistician and Data Analyst with a passion for using data to solve real-world problems in agriculture, ecology, and environmental science. My work focuses on transforming complex research data into meaningful insights that support scientific discovery and evidence-based decision-making.

I have several years of experience supporting agricultural and ecological research through data management, statistical modelling, visualization, and reporting. Although my academic background is in Biological and Ecological Sciences, I have developed strong analytical and programming skills through practical experience and continuous self-learning.

I am currently expanding my expertise in Data Science, Machine Learning, Database Systems, and Artificial Intelligence to build modern analytical solutions for agricultural research.
