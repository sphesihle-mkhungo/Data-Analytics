# 🗺️ Roadmap

Welcome to the development roadmap for the **Agricultural Data Science Toolkit**.

This roadmap outlines the planned development of the toolkit as it evolves into a comprehensive open-source educational resource for agricultural data science, statistics, GIS, and research computing.

---

# Vision

To create a freely available, high-quality toolkit that helps students, researchers, and professionals learn and apply data science in agriculture, ecology, environmental science, and biological research.
